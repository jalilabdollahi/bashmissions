(( )) works as an if condition: it is true when the expression is true. Inside it you can use <, <=, >, >=, == and != just like in maths, and * for arithmetic. Generic example:

  if (( temperature * 10 > limit * 9 )); then
    echo "hot"
  elif (( temperature >= 20 )); then
    echo "warm"
  else
    echo "cold"
  fi
