Three options do the work:

  -t,          the separator is a comma
  -k2,2n       key = field 2 only, numeric
  -k2,2nr      same, but biggest first (r = reverse, for this key only)

Several -k options are tried in order, so the second one breaks ties:

  sort -t: -k4,4n -k1,1 /etc/example     # by field 4 as a number, then by field 1

Write the end of the key (",2") or sort will compare from field 2 to the end of the line.
