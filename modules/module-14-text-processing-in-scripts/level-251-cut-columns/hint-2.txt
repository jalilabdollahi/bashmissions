The tool is cut. Two options matter:

  cut -d: -f1,3 /etc/example     # delimiter ':', keep fields 1 and 3
  cut -d' ' -f2 words.txt        # a space delimiter must be quoted
  cut -d, -f2-4 data.csv         # a range: fields 2 through 4

Fields are counted from 1, and the kept ones come out joined by the same delimiter.
