A bracket expression with a caret at the front means "any character NOT in this list". Used in a replace-all with an empty replacement, it deletes everything else:

  word="b-a-n-a-n-a"
  dashes=${word//[^-]/}     # "-----"
  echo "${#dashes}"         # 5

You can only apply one expansion at a time, so store the intermediate result in a variable.
