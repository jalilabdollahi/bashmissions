Put a hash or a percent sign right after the first slash of the replace expansion:

  f="draft-notes-draft"
  echo "${f/#draft/final}"     # final-notes-draft  (only at the start)
  echo "${f/%draft/final}"     # draft-notes-final  (only at the end)

Slashes inside the pattern or the replacement must be escaped with a backslash, or put the strings in variables first and use those.
