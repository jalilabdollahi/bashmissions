Parentheses in a regex capture whatever they matched. After a successful [[ =~ ]], the whole match is ${BASH_REMATCH[0]} and the groups are ${BASH_REMATCH[1]}, [2], and so on, numbered by their opening bracket:

  re='^user=([a-z]+) uid=([0-9]+)$'
  if [[ $row =~ $re ]]; then
    echo "${BASH_REMATCH[1]} has id ${BASH_REMATCH[2]}"
  fi

Handy pieces: [A-Z]+ capitals, [^ ]+ any non-space characters, [0-9]{3} exactly three digits.
