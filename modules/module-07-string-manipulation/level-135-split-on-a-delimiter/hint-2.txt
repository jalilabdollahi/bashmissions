`read -a` stores the pieces in an indexed array, and setting IFS just for that command picks the delimiter:

  row="north:pier:dental"
  IFS=: read -ra part <<< "$row"
  echo "${part[0]}"     # north
  echo "${part[2]}"     # dental

`<<<` (here-string) feeds the text to read. Count the fields of the passwd line and subtract one to get the index.
