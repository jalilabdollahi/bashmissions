Square brackets match ONE character out of a set. Inside them you can write a range or a named POSIX class:

  [a-f]          one of a b c d e f
  [[:digit:]]    one digit
  [[:upper:]]    one capital letter

Put each one where one character is expected, anchor the ends, and keep the regex in a variable:

  re='^id-[A-Z][[:digit:]]$'     # id-Q7
