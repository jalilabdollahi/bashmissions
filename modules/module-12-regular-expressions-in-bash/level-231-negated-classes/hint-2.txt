A caret as the first character inside brackets negates the class:

  [^ ]      any one character except a space
  [^,]*     zero or more characters that are not commas
  [^"]*     zero or more characters that are not a double quote

A match for [^"]* can never run over a quote, so it stops at the next one. Wrap it in a group to capture it, and keep the regex in a single-quoted variable so the " is no problem:

  re='name="([^"]*)"'
