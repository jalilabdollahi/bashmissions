Useful building blocks:

  [[:alnum:]._%+-]+   one or more letters, digits or any of . _ % + -   (put - LAST in the brackets)
  \.                  a literal dot
  [[:alpha:]]{2,}     two or more letters
  ^ and $             the ends of the whole string

Keep the finished regex in a variable and test with [[ $addr =~ $re ]].
