In sed -E, parentheses capture and the replacement refers to them by number: \1 is the first group, \2 the second, and so on:

  echo 'ab-12' | sed -E 's/([a-z]+)-([0-9]+)/\2:\1/'      # prints 12:ab

Add the g flag to treat every date on a line. If the pattern itself contains slashes, give s another delimiter, for example |
