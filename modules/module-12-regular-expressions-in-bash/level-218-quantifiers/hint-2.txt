Quantifiers apply to the single item just before them (a character, a class, or a group in parentheses):

  +        one or more        [a-z]+
  *        zero or more       [ ]*
  ?        zero or one        (ab)?
  {3}      exactly three      [0-9]{3}
  {2,4}    two to four        [0-9]{2,4}

Parentheses group several characters so a quantifier can apply to all of them: (-x[0-9])?
