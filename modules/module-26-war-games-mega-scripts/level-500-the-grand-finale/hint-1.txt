Write the pieces as functions that print their lines and set counters, then let the commands combine them. `health` and `backups` each call their function and add a summary line; `report` calls both and turns the counters into a verdict; `handover` calls the report silently and only proceeds when the verdict is GREEN. That way the logic exists once and every command stays small. Treat the date as a problem of its own: you need to validate a YYYYMMDD value and to subtract two dates, and without `date -d` you do that with a little integer arithmetic.
