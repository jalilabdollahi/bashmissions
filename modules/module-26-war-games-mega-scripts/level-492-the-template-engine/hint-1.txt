Work in two phases. First read the template once and decide what each line is: ordinary text, or one of the directive lines (if, else, endif, for, endfor). Use a stack to pair every opener with its closer and remember the line numbers; this is where you detect all the structural mistakes. Second, render: a function that renders a range of lines can call itself for the body of an if or a for, which is what makes nesting work. Put the output into a variable, not on the screen, so that an error half-way through prints nothing at all.
