Think of it as repeatedly peeling the graph. Everything that needs nothing can start now: that is layer 1. Once those are running, some services have no unmet needs left: layer 2, and so on. For that you need to know, for each service, how many of its dependencies are still unplaced, and for each service, who is waiting for it, so that placing a service can release its dependents. If at some point nothing is ready but services remain, they all wait for each other: a cycle. Do the parsing and the error reporting before any of this.
