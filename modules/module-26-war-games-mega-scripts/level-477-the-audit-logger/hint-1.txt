The heart of the tool is one function that walks every segment in order and either returns the final chain state (last sequence number and last checksum) or the exact place where the chain breaks. Every command then becomes a thin layer: `verify` prints the result of the walk, `append` and `checkpoint` and `rotate` refuse to run when the walk fails and otherwise use the final state, `search` asks the walk first and filters afterwards. Think separately about what the chain can prove (nothing was edited in the middle) and what it cannot (something was cut off the end), and let the anchor cover the second part.
