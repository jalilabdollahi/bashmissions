The live release is not a folder, it is a *pointer* (a symlink called current). Switching releases means changing the pointer, and changing it must never leave a moment when the pointer is missing or half-made. So: build the new pointer next to the old one under another name, and only then swap names in a single rename. Before the swap run the smoke test on the idle folder; after the swap remember what you left, so rollback is just another swap. Write small helpers: find the live slot, read a version, point current at a slot.
