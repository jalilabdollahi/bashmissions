Treat time as a row of minutes. A service's window starts at its first probe and ends at its last, and every minute in between is either "up" (a good probe) or "bad" (a down probe, or no probe at all because the robot was blind). From that row you can read everything: total minutes, bad minutes, the runs of consecutive bad minutes. So process the probe log in order, and for each service remember only the last minute seen, the number of bad minutes and the length of the current bad run. Work in whole numbers and scale percentages by 1000.
