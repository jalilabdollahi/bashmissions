Three kinds of records, one folder: a sorted file of counters, an append-only log of lines, and an append-only list of spans (a span is "this step, started then, lasted this long, ended like this"). The only clever part is the span's family tree: when a span runs a command that itself starts a span, the child has to learn the trace it belongs to and which span is its parent. Environment variables are the way a parent hands information to everything it starts. The report then rebuilds the tree from the flat list: group spans by parent, print roots first and each span's children underneath it.
