An invoice is three small calculations in a row: price a line, add the lines up, then adjust the total (discount, minimum, tax). Do every one of them on integers: store prices in ten-thousandths of a dollar, round each item line to whole cents once, and from that point only touch cents. Aggregation first: read the CSV and add up the quantity per customer-and-item pair in an associative array, then print customers in sorted order and items in the price list's order. Graduated tiers are the only real puzzle: charge each slice of the quantity at its own tier's price.
