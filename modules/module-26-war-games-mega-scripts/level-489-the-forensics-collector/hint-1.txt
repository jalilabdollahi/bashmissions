Two jobs, one idea: a fingerprint written down at collection time can be compared with a fingerprint computed later. collect does everything that must happen before anyone analyses anything: refuse to overwrite, copy, compare the copy with the source, lock the copy, write the manifest of fingerprints, then fingerprint the manifest itself (the seal) so the list of fingerprints cannot be edited unnoticed. verify replays those fingerprints and reports every way the folder can disagree with the manifest. Keep the custody log append-only: every action adds a line, nothing rewrites it.
