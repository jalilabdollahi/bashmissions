Inside [[ ]], the right-hand side of == is a pattern when you do not quote it. * matches any run of characters (even none) and ? matches exactly one. Generic example:

  if [[ $file == backup-* ]]; then
    echo "starts with backup-"
  elif [[ $file == *.zip ]]; then
    echo "ends with .zip"
  elif [[ $file == img-??.png ]]; then
    echo "img- plus two characters"
  fi
