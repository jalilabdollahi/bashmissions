`$@` means "all the arguments". Its quoted form `"$@"` expands to each argument as its own properly quoted word:

  ./wrapper.sh "a b" c     # two arguments
  other-tool "$@"          # other-tool receives "a b" and c
  other-tool $@            # other-tool receives a, b and c: three!

Always use the quoted form when passing arguments on.
