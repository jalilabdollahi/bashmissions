The classic skeleton uses `while`, `case` and `shift`:

  while [[ $# -gt 0 ]]; do
    case $1 in
      --name)  name=$2; shift 2 ;;    # takes a value: consume two
      --loud)  loud=yes; shift ;;     # a switch: consume one
      *)       echo "bad: $1" >&2; exit 2 ;;
    esac
  done

`*)` is the catch-all branch, and `;;` ends each branch.
