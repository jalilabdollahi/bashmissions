Use `read` as the condition of a `while` loop, and update counters in the body with `$(( ))` arithmetic:

  count=0
  while read -r word number; do
    count=$((count + 1))
  done
  echo "$count"

A final line without a newline makes `read` fail even though it received text, so extend the condition: `|| [[ -n $number ]]`.
