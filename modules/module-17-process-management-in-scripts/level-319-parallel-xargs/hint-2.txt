`xargs -n 1 -P 4 cmd` reads words from stdin, runs `cmd word` once per word, and keeps up to 4 running at once. If any call fails, xargs exits with 123. With `set -o pipefail`, a pipeline fails when any part of it fails, so you can translate that into your own exit status:

  printf '%s\n' a b c | xargs -n 1 -P 2 echo | sort
  if ! xargs ... | sort; then exit 1; fi

The number of workers can come from a variable: `-P "$workers"`.
