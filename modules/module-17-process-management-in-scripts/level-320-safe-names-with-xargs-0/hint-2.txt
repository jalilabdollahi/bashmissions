`find ... -print0` ends every name with a NUL byte instead of a newline, and `xargs -0` splits its input on NUL bytes only, doing no quote or space processing at all:

  find photos -type f -name '*.jpg' -print0 | xargs -0 ls -l

`find -type f` matches regular files only (not folders), and `-name '*.log'` needs its quotes so the shell does not expand the star itself.
