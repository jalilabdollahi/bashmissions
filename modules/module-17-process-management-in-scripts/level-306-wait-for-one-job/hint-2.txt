`wait PID` blocks until that process ends and then returns its exit status, so `$?` (or an `||` branch) tells you how it went:

  sleep 1 &
  pid=$!
  wait "$pid"
  echo "sleep ended with $?"

If your script uses `set -e`, a non-zero `wait` stops it. Write `status=0` first and then `wait "$pid" || status=$?` to keep control.
