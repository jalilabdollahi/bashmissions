`$$` is the PID of the running script. `kill -USR1 $$` sends it signal USR1 (a signal with no built-in meaning, free for your own use), and `trap handler USR1` registers `handler` (a function or a quoted command) to run when it arrives:

  hello() { echo "got a signal"; }
  trap hello USR1
  kill -USR1 $$        # prints: got a signal

USR1 and USR2 are the two "user defined" signals.
