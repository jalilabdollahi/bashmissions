`mkfifo name` creates a named pipe. Anything written into it can be read out of it, in order. Start the writer with `&`, and let a loop read from the pipe by redirecting the loop's input:

  mkfifo demo.pipe
  producer demo.pipe &
  while IFS= read -r line; do
    echo "got: $line"
  done < demo.pipe
  rm demo.pipe

The loop ends by itself when the writer closes the pipe.
