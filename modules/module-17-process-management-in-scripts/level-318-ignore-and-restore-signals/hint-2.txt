`trap '' SIGNAL` (an empty command) makes the shell ignore the signal. `trap - SIGNAL` restores the default action. Children inherit an ignored signal, but not a handler:

  trap '' TERM
  kill -TERM $$        # survived
  trap - TERM
  kill -TERM $$        # the script is terminated here

To keep a non-zero status from ending a `set -e` script, write `status=0; cmd || status=$?`.
