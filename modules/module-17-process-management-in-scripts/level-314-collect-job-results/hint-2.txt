`mktemp -d` creates a new empty folder and prints its name. Number your jobs and use the number as the file name, then redirect both output and errors into it:

  tmp=$(mktemp -d)
  some_command > "$tmp/1" 2>&1 &
  wait
  cat "$tmp/1"
  rm -rf "$tmp"

`n=$((n + 1))` counts, and `for ((i = 1; i <= n; i++))` visits the files in order.
