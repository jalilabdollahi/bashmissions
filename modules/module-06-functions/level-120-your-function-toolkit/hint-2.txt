Reuse what the module taught:
  - die: shift off the code, print to stderr, exit "$code"
  - slugify: printf '%s' "$1" | tr 'A-Z' 'a-z' | tr -cs 'a-z0-9' '-' | sed -E 's/^-+//; s/-+$//'
    (tr -cs squeezes every run of other characters into a single hyphen)
  - valid_quota: a regex test for digits, then a numeric range test, joined with &&
  - tier_for: if/elif that echoes a word, captured with $( )
Put the helpers first, the flow last.
