Go from oldest to newest: `rm -f` the last one, then `mv` the second-to-last onto the last place, and so on. A small loop with arithmetic in the name keeps it short, and `[[ -e path ]]` guards a step that may have nothing to move:

  for n in 2 1; do
    [[ -e "$f.$n" ]] && mv "$f.$n" "$f.$((n + 1))"
  done
