`${VAR:-}` is safe under `set -u` even when VAR is not set. A glob test checks the first character:

  dir=${SOME_DIR:-}
  if [[ $dir != /* ]]; then dir=$HOME/fallback; fi

An array keeps candidates in order: `list=("$a" "$b")` and `for p in "${list[@]}"`. `[[ -f $p ]]` is true for regular files only. `${p#"$HOME"}` removes a leading $HOME from p.
