`compgen -v PREFIX` lists the names of all variables that start with PREFIX (add `|| true` under `set -e`, it fails when there is none). `${!name}` reads a variable by name and `${name,,}` lowercases. Associative arrays remember nothing about order, so sort their keys:

  declare -A seen=([b]=2 [a]=1)
  printf '%s\n' "${!seen[@]}" | sort

`printf '%-*s' "$width" "$text"` pads `text` on the right to `width` characters.
