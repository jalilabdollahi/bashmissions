Regex groups from `[[ ... =~ ... ]]` land in BASH_REMATCH:

  if [[ $line =~ ^(yes|no)[[:space:]]+([0-9]+) ]]; then
    word=${BASH_REMATCH[1]}; id=${BASH_REMATCH[2]}
  fi

Write "not ok" with a backslash-escaped space (`not\ ok`) inside an unquoted regex, or keep the pattern in a variable. `${text%% #*}` removes everything from the first " #" onward.
