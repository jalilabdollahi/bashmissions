The classic loop:

  while [ "$#" -gt 0 ]; do
    case "$1" in
      -v|--verbose) verbose=1; shift ;;
      -o|--output)  out=$2; shift 2 ;;
      --)           shift; break ;;
      -*)           echo "unknown option: $1" >&2; exit 2 ;;
      *)            break ;;
    esac
  done

`shift` drops `$1`; `shift 2` drops a flag and its value. After the loop, `"$@"` holds only the positionals. Check `$#` before reading `$2`, since an option may be last.
