A function that prints several lines is easiest with a here-document:

  show() {
    cat <<'TXT'
  line one
  line two
  TXT
  }

Redirect a call like any command: `show >&2`. Count arguments with `$#`, and compare numbers with `[ "$#" -ne 2 ]`. Under `set -u`, peeking at a maybe-missing first argument needs `${1:-}`.
