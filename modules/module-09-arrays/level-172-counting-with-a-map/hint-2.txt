After declare -A seen, the line seen[$k]=$(( ${seen[$k]:-0} + 1 )) adds one to the counter for key $k, treating a missing key as 0. To print in order, send the keys through sort: printf '%s\n' "${!seen[@]}" | sort lists them alphabetically, and you can read them back with a while read loop fed by < <( ... ). Guard the empty map, because printf '%s\n' with no keys prints a blank line.
