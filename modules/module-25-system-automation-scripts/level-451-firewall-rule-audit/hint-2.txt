In awk, `$1..$NF` are the fields and `NR` is the line number. Loop `for (i = 2; i <= NF; i++)` and when `$i == "-s"` store `$(i+1)`. Do the same for `-i`, `-p`, `-m`, `--dport`/`--dports` and `-j`. Reset your variables at the start of every rule. `split(ports, p, ",")` breaks a multiport list into elements, and `index(p[k], ":")` finds ranges. `/^-A INPUT /` and `/^:INPUT /` select the lines you care about, and `END { ...; exit (found > 0) }` sets the exit status.
