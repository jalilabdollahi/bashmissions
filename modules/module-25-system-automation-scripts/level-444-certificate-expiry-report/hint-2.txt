openssl prints `notAfter=Mar  4 12:00:00 2026 GMT`. Strip the prefix with `${line#notAfter=}` and split with `read -r mon day time year zone <<< "..."` (read splits on runs of blanks, so the double space is harmless). Convert the month name with a lookup string or a `case`. Collect lines like `14 www`, then `sort -n` orders them, negative numbers included. `printf '%-10s %5d  %s\n'` makes the columns.
