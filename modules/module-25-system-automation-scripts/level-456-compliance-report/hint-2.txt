`while getopts "fm:" opt; do case $opt in f) ...;; m) minimum=$OPTARG;; esac; done; shift $((OPTIND - 1))` handles `-f` and `-m PCT` and leaves the folder as `$1`. A function's exit status is its answer: `check_x() { [[ -f $1/file ]] && grep -q pattern "$1/file"; }` and then `if check_x "$dir"; then ...`. Loop over host folders with `for d in "$root"/*/`. In awk, `$5 + 0` turns `97%` into 97, `tolower($1)` handles any capitalisation. Integer percentage: `$(( passed * 100 / total ))`.
