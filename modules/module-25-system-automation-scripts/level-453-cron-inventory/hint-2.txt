`read -r a b c d e rest <<< "$line"` assigns the first five whitespace-separated words to a..e and everything left (kept verbatim, only the edges trimmed) to `rest`; extra blanks and tabs between fields disappear. Recognise lines to skip with regexes: `^[[:space:]]*(#.*)?$` and `^[[:space:]]*[A-Za-z_][A-Za-z0-9_]*=`. For `@reboot cmd`, `read -r first rest` is enough. A glob `"$dir"/*` is sorted and skips dot-files; test `[[ -f $path ]]`. Align columns with `printf '%-10s %-17s %s\n'`.
