`set -o pipefail` (part of `set -euo pipefail`) makes `pg_dump ... | gzip > file` fail when pg_dump fails. Use it as an `if` condition: `if ! pg_dump --no-owner "$db" | gzip > "$partial"; then rm -f "$partial"; ...; exit 1; fi`. Verify with `gzip -t "$partial"` and publish with `mv`. For pruning, a glob such as `"$outdir/$db"-[0-9]*.sql.gz` expands in sorted order (oldest first) and ignores other databases whose names merely start the same way; with `shopt -s nullglob` an empty match gives an empty array.
